Version 1.5.1 [2026 First Release] Changelog

Released on September 16, 2026

Improved

  • Default lists will no longer include content by default. We noticed that people often assumed these lists would be automatically tailored to their needs. When something didn't work for them, they would sometimes remove the plugin instead of adjusting the list, likely because they weren't familiar with how the tool worked. By having users create their own lists, we hope to make the tool's customization clearer and encourage people to take a moment to understand how it works and make it fit their needs.
